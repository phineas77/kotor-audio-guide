Frequently asked questions

How much of the guide can I try for free?

The first audio story in each of the eight languages is free to listen to.

Does buying one language unlock the others?

No. Each language is a separate one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchasing one language unlocks the remaining 28 stories in that language only. There are no subscriptions or all-languages bundles.

Can I use the audio guide offline?

Yes. Audio recordings are included in the app. Once your purchase has been recognized, you can listen to that language offline. The map and external directions may require an internet connection.

How do I restore a purchase?

  1. Open Kotor Audio Guide and select the language you purchased.
  2. Open a locked story to view the purchase screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases while connected to the internet.
  4. Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account that was used for the original purchase.

If restoration does not work, contact support with the product language and the date of purchase. Do not share your Apple or Google password.

Can I transfer a purchase from iPhone to Android or vice versa?

Purchases are handled by the relevant app store. Purchases made through Apple's App Store are not automatically transferable to Google Play, or vice versa.

How do I request a refund?

For purchases made on an iPhone, visit Apple's Report a Problem. For purchases made through Google Play, use Google Play's refund instructions. Refund eligibility and decisions are handled by the stores.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Kotor Audio Guide does not require a user registration or login.

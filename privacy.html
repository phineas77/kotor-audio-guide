Privacy Policy

How Kotor Audio Guide handles app preferences, purchases, maps, and support requests.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Who operates the app

Kotor Audio Guide (the “App”) is developed and operated by Veljko Dudić, an independent developer based in Montenegro (“we”, “us”, “our”). For privacy questions or requests, email veljkodudic7@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Kotor Audio Guide provides self-guided audio stories, maps showing points of interest in Kotor, Montenegro, and optional one-time purchases of individual narration languages. The audio recordings are bundled with the installed app; there is no separate audio-streaming account or service.

You do not need to create an account or give us your name or email address to use the App.

3. Information processed

Preferences and listening progress (stored on your device)

The App stores your selected language, onboarding state, and which stories you have listened to on your device. This information supports your listening experience and is not intentionally sent to our own server.

Purchase and entitlement information

To offer, validate, and restore individual language purchases, we use Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat. The App generates a random app-specific customer identifier, which is stored on the device and supplied to RevenueCat. RevenueCat and the applicable app store may process transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, products purchased, entitlement status, timestamps, and technical information such as device or operating-system details and IP address to operate the purchase service and prevent misuse. We do not receive your full payment-card number.

Maps and links to external services

The App displays a map of predetermined Kotor landmarks using a device map provider, including Google Maps where configured. Map providers may receive technical or usage information, including IP address, needed to deliver map content. If you open a location or walking directions in Google Maps, that external service processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The App does not require continuous GPS tracking or your precise live location to play audio stories.

Support correspondence

If you contact us for support, we process the email address and information you choose to include in your message, so we can respond and resolve your request.

Website visits

These Support and Privacy Policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver the website. We do not include third-party ad tracking or analytics scripts on these pages.

4. How and why information is used

  • App operation: remember selected language and listening progress.
  • Purchases: process and verify payments, recognize purchased languages, and restore purchases. These operations are needed to provide a purchased service.
  • Maps: display landmark locations and provide optional external directions.
  • Support: answer questions, diagnose issues, and respond to privacy requests.
  • Security and compliance: protect purchase systems, address misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable under data-protection law, these purposes rely on performing a contract with you, our legitimate interests in providing a secure and functional app, or compliance with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

5. Service providers and external policies

Depending on which functions you use, personal or technical information may be processed by third parties:

  • Apple: App Store purchases, purchase restoration, and refunds. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google: map delivery, optional external directions, and Google Play purchases on Android. Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification and language entitlements. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub: hosting of these static website pages. GitHub Privacy Statement.

These providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, under their applicable policies and safeguards.

6. How long information is kept

Device preferences and listening progress are kept locally according to device and app storage behavior. Purchase records may be retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as needed to provide access, document transactions, and meet their retention and legal obligations. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to make any purchase and listen to the first story in each language for free. You may contact us to ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we control, subject to applicable law and any required record retention. For purchases and payment information held by Apple or Google, you may also need to use your app-store account privacy controls. You can manage device-level permissions through your device settings.

Privacy contact: veljkodudic7@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

The App is designed as a general-interest travel and cultural guide, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal contact details directly in the App. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Security and changes to this policy

We use the operating system's on-device storage features and established app-store and purchase-service providers. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy when the App or our data practices change; the updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.
